## Selection Process

The selection of candidates for the MP Police Constable position involves multiple stages:

1. **Written Exam:** To test the candidate's general knowledge, reasoning, and fundamental skills.
2. **Physical Test:** Physical Proficiency Test (PPT) to evaluate the candidate's physical fitness.
3. **Document Verification:** Verification of all relevant educational and identity documents.
4. **Medical Examination:** Final medical fitness test before engagement.
